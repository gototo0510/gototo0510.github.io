Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-29

GOTOTO Music Tools uses TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API.

The app may access basic TikTok profile information such as Open ID, avatar URL, and display name through the user.info.basic scope.

The app may upload user-selected MP4 files to the authenticated user's TikTok account as drafts through the video.upload scope.

Access tokens are stored only for the purpose of operating the tool. The app does not sell user data and does not share user data with advertisers.

The app does not collect passwords. TikTok authentication is handled through TikTok OAuth.
