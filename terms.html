Last updated: 2026-05-29

GOTOTO Music Tools is an internal content management tool for the GOTOTO music project.

The tool is used to sign in with TikTok, check basic profile information, and upload original GOTOTO short-form music videos as drafts using the TikTok Content Posting API.

Users must only upload videos they own or have the right to publish.

This service does not use scraping, browser automation, Selenium, Puppeteer, or unofficial TikTok posting methods.
